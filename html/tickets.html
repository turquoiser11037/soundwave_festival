Tickets

Important Information

If you have bought a ticket for one day, you will be able to see any performances past the day in the night time.

e.g. if you bought a Friday ticket, you get to see the performances on Saturday morning - up to 04:00

Frequently Asked Questions

If I'm bringing any children, what will their ticket price be?

Children aged 0-7 go free

Children aged 8-12 get 20% off ticket prices

Teenagers aged 13-17 get 10% off ticket prices

Please note that all children aged 0-15 have to attend with a responsible adult.
